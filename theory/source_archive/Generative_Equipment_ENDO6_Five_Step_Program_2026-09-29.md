## 2. ENDO-6B — Actualization Frontier Theory

Let
\[
C:\mathcal A\to\mathcal F
\]
be the actual/formal comparison.

For \(y\in\mathcal F\), define the object actualization fiber
\[
\operatorname{Eff}_C(y):=\operatorname{hofib}_y(C^\simeq:\mathcal A^\simeq\to\mathcal F^\simeq).
\]

Define the actualization frontier as the full subcategory of \(\mathcal F\) spanned by objects with empty actualization fiber:
\[
\operatorname{Front}(C)=\{y:\operatorname{Eff}_C(y)=\varnothing\}.
\]

### Frontier theorem
\[
\operatorname{Front}(C)=\varnothing
\iff C\text{ is essentially surjective}.
\]
If \(C\) is fully faithful, then the empty frontier condition is equivalent to \(C\) being an equivalence.

For morphisms \(\phi:C(a)\to C(b)\), define
\[
\operatorname{Eff}^{\rm map}_C(\phi)=\operatorname{hofib}_\phi\bigl(\operatorname{Map}_\mathcal A(a,b)\to\operatorname{Map}_\mathcal F(Ca,Cb)\bigr).
\]
This separates object frontier from mapping/coherence frontier.

## 4. ENDO-6D — Deformation-sector instantiation

Let \(L\) be a DGLA over a characteristic-zero field \(k\).  For a finite set \(J\), set
\[
B_J=k[\varepsilon_i\mid i\in J]/(\varepsilon_i^2).
\]
Fix closed singleton directions \(v_i\in Z^1(L)\).

A full Maurer–Cartan coefficient system is
\[
x=\sum_{\varnothing\neq S\subseteq J}x_S\varepsilon_S,
\qquad x_{\{i\}}=v_i,
\]
with \(dx+\frac12[x,x]=0\).

Let \(\mathsf{Formal}_J(L;v)\) be the punctured systems with coefficients only for proper nonempty \(S\subsetneq J\), satisfying every proper-support MC equation.  Let \(\mathsf{Actual}_J(L;v)\) be the full systems, and let
\[
C_J:\mathsf{Actual}_J(L;v)\to\mathsf{Formal}_J(L;v)
\]
forget the top coefficient.

For a punctured datum define
\[
\Omega_J=\frac12\sum_{A\sqcup B=J}[x_A,x_B].
\]
Proper equations imply \(d\Omega_J=0\).

### Punctured-cube frontier theorem
The fiber over a punctured datum is
\[
\{x_J\in L^1:dx_J=-\Omega_J\}.
\]
Therefore
\[
 x_\partial\in\operatorname{Front}(C_J)
 \iff [\Omega_J(x_\partial)]\neq0\in H^2(L).
\]
When nonempty, the raw top-coefficient fiber is an affine torsor over \(Z^1(L)\).

Define the saturated direction obstruction image
\[
\mathfrak O_J(v)=\{[\Omega_J(x_\partial)]:x_\partial\in\mathsf{Formal}_J(L;v)\}\subseteq H^2(L).
\]
Then
\[
(v_i)_{i\in J}\text{ is simultaneously realizable}\iff0\in\mathfrak O_J(v).
\]

For \(|J|=4\), the previous \(2+2\) threshold makes this obstruction image genuinely nonlinear in pair-filler choices.

The frontier compiler therefore asks:

> Can the first nonlinear \(2+2\) threshold produce an intrinsic failure in which every proper subfamily is realizable but the full four-direction tuple is not, even after all lower choices are saturated?

## 5. ENDO-6E — First frontier-generated theorem

### Theorem — Intrinsic nonlinear four-direction actualization failure

Let \(k\) be any field of characteristic zero.  Define a DGLA \(L\) by
\[
L^0=0,
\]
\[
L^1=\langle v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4,e_{12},e_{34}\rangle_k,
\]
\[
L^2=\langle p_{12},p_{34},w\rangle_k,
\]
and all other degrees zero.

Differential:
\[
dv_i=0,\qquad de_{12}=p_{12},\qquad de_{34}=p_{34},\qquad dp_{12}=dp_{34}=dw=0.
\]

The only nonzero basic brackets are
\[
[v_1,v_2]=-p_{12},\qquad [v_3,v_4]=-p_{34},\qquad[e_{12},e_{34}]=w,
\]
with \(L^2\) central.

This is a two-step nilpotent DGLA: all Jacobiators vanish because every bracket lands centrally; the differential derivation rule is immediate on the first two brackets and for the third gives
\[
d[e_{12},e_{34}]=0=[p_{12},e_{34}]-[e_{12},p_{34}].
\]

#### Every proper subfamily is realizable
For pair \(12\), choose \(x_{12}=e_{12}\); for pair \(34\), choose \(x_{34}=e_{34}\); all other pair coefficients may be zero.  No proper subset of four labels contains both complete pairs \(12\) and \(34\).  All singleton-pair brackets involving \(e_{12}\) or \(e_{34}\) vanish, so all triple coefficients may be taken zero.  Hence every proper subfamily is MC-realizable.

#### Full saturation cannot remove the obstruction
For an arbitrary compatible punctured four-cube, the pair equations force
\[
x_{12}=e_{12}+z_{12},\qquad x_{34}=e_{34}+z_{34},
\]
with \(z_{12},z_{34}\in Z^1(L)\).  Since
\[
Z^1(L)=\langle v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4\rangle,
\]
all other pair fillers are also linear combinations of the \(v_i\)'s.

The top curvature is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Omega_4={}&[v_1,x_{234}]+[v_2,x_{134}]+[v_3,x_{124}]+[v_4,x_{123}]\\
&+[x_{12},x_{34}]+[x_{13},x_{24}]+[x_{14},x_{23}].
\end{aligned}
\]
Every singleton-triple term and the last two pair-pair terms is a combination of \(p_{12},p_{34}\), hence exact.  Moreover
\[
[x_{12},x_{34}]=w+[z_{12},z_{34}],
\]
and \([z_{12},z_{34}]\) is again a combination of \(p_{12},p_{34}\), hence exact.

Therefore for every compatible punctured four-cube
\[
[\Omega_4]=[w].
\]
But
\[
\operatorname{im}(d:L^1\to L^2)=\langle p_{12},p_{34}\rangle,
\]
so \([w]\neq0\in H^2(L)\).  Hence
\[
\boxed{\mathfrak O_4(v)=\{[w]\}},
\]
and no full four-variable MC element exists.

Thus every proper subfamily is realizable, while the full tuple is intrinsically and nonlinearly obstructed after complete saturation.

## 6. Research-value audit

- **Saturation:** the result is not a bad chosen boundary; all compatible proper-face choices are included and the obstruction class remains \([w]\).
- **Theory-generation:** the problem was generated by the actual/formal comparison and the nonempty saturated frontier, not inserted as a target answer.
- **Theory-independent statement:** the DGLA theorem is meaningful without Generative Equipment vocabulary.
- **Literature boundary:** standard DGLA obstruction theory already places obstructions in \(H^2\), and nonlinear Kuranishi relations are standard in higher deformation theory.  No claim of literature-first originality is made for this 9-dimensional example without a dedicated literature audit.
